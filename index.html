About the App

AcademicLens-AI is an advanced academic study and research assistant powered by artificial intelligence. It is designed to help students and researchers analyze complex scientific papers, summarize academic lessons, extract key insights, and elevate writing efficiency seamlessly.

Help & Guidelines

1. Select any academic tool card from the main dashboard.

2. Upload a research PDF paper, image diagram, or paste your lesson notes.

3. Customize options like answer length or academic style when available.

4. Click the action button to process and view AI outputs instantly.
